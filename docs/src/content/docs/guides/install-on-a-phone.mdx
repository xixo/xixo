---
title: Install xixo on a phone
description: Add xixo to a phone's home screen and share files, links, and text to it.
---

At the end of this guide, xixo opens from your phone's home screen in a window of its own. On
Android, it is also listed in the share sheet. A phone installs xixo only over `https`, so open your
xixo's public origin. See [self-hosting](/guides/self-hosting/).

## Install on Android

1. Open your xixo in Chrome and sign in.
2. Open the menu and choose **Add to home screen**.
3. Choose **Install**.

## Install on an iPhone or iPad

1. Open your xixo in Safari and sign in.
2. Choose the share button, then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Choose **Add**.

Safari does not list installed web apps in the share sheet, so sharing to xixo works on Android
only.

When the phone is offline or the server does not answer, the installed app shows **xixo is out of
reach**. It opens again when the phone is back online, or when you choose **Try again**.

## Share to xixo

1. In another app, choose **Share**.
2. Choose **xixo**.

xixo opens and says what it kept:

- A file is added the way a dropped file is, and xixo opens on its item. A file already in the
  catalog is not stored again.
- A link to a page is rendered when a `web` resource is attached. A link to a file, such as a PDF,
  is fetched into storage.
- Text, and a link that cannot be rendered or fetched, is kept as a note. The note is named by the
  shared title, or by its first line.

The same limits apply as for an upload. See [staging](/concepts/adding/#staging).

## If a share does not land

- **The sign-in page opens.** The phone was not signed in, and the share was not kept. Sign in,
  then share again.
- **Your sign-in may read the catalog but not add to it.** Sharing needs `xixo:catalog:write`.
- **That share could not be kept.** No attached storage accepts it. See
  [attach a resource](/guides/attach-a-resource/).
